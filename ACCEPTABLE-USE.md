# Acceptable use

**This is the lab's position, not a licence term.** What binds you is [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) —
PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 — and nothing on this page adds to it or takes anything away. It is
here so the expectation is written down rather than assumed.

These skills produce things people watch, read and are persuaded by: films, decks, reports,
narration in a voice that sounds like someone. That is the whole point of them, and it is also
why the ways they can be misused are worth naming.

## What we ask you not to do with them

- **Do not make a voice say something the person never agreed to say.** `natural-voice` clones
  timbre. Clone your own, or one you have permission for, and say that it is synthetic when the
  listener would otherwise assume a person spoke.
- **Do not present generated footage or audio as a record of something that happened.** These
  skills make explanations, not evidence.
- **Do not use them to harass, impersonate, or deceive** — including passing off someone else's
  project as your own work, which is the academic version of the same thing.
- **Do not use them for sexual content involving minors, or to produce material that exists to
  degrade a person or a group.** This should not need saying; it is said anyway.

## What we ask you to do

- **Keep the honesty rules.** Every skill in here refuses to claim what the work has not done —
  a report that has not been tested says so, a deck does not assert a number it cannot source.
  That constraint is most of what makes the output worth trusting. Removing it is permitted by
  the licence and is still the wrong thing to do.
- **Say a machine helped.** Not as an apology, and not in the artifact itself if that is awkward
  — but somewhere the reader can find.
- **Send the friction back.** The loop in [`feedback/`](feedback/README.md) is how these get
  better, and a run that went wrong somewhere else teaches more than one that went right here.

## If you think something here is being misused

Open an issue, or contact the lab. We would rather hear about it than not.
